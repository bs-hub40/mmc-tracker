Privacy

MMC Tracker is a client-side nutrition and activity log.

  • Your meals, activity, weight, and settings are stored in this browser.
  • If you sign in with Google, a copy is saved in a folder named MMC Tracker on your Google Drive. We do not operate a server that stores your log.
  • AI parsing uses an API key you provide. That key stays in your browser (and Drive backup). We do not collect it.
  • We do not sell your data. We do not show ads.

You can delete Drive data by removing the MMC Tracker folder, and sign out any time.
