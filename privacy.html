Privacy

Log it is a client-side food, activity, and weight tracker.

  • You sign in with Google. Meals, activity, weight, and settings stay in this browser, and a copy is saved in a folder named Log it on your Google Drive. We do not operate a server that stores your log.
  • AI parsing is included. Meal and workout text is sent to Log it's server to estimate macros, then discarded. We do not use that text to train models.
  • We do not sell your data. We do not show ads.

You can delete Drive data by removing the Log it folder, and sign out any time.
